    14 Days | Roots & Reconnection Family Journey

    Back to Sri Lanka

    Duration14 days / 13 nights
    FromUS$1,816
    StyleFamily · heritage · north & east
    Tailor this journey

    Overview

    Reconnect with Sri Lanka through a meaningful family journey combining heritage, culture, wildlife, scenic landscapes and authentic local experiences. Travel from Colombo to the southern coast, experience a Yala wildlife adventure, explore Ella and Nuwara Eliya’s misty tea country, and discover the cultural treasures of Kandy and Sigiriya.

    Continue through Trincomalee to Jaffna, where traditional fishing, palmyrah toddy tasting, pottery making and a hands-on cooking experience offer a deeper connection to Northern Sri Lankan life.

    Highlights

    • Reconnect with Sri Lankan roots on a meaningful family journey
    • Explore the colonial heritage and oceanfront beauty of Galle Fort
    • Enjoy relaxing family time on Mirissa’s tropical beaches
    • Experience an exciting wildlife safari in Yala National Park
    • Discover Ella’s Nine Arch Bridge and relax at Ravana Pool Club
    • Travel through tea country aboard Sri Lanka’s famous scenic train
    • Visit Ramboda Falls and a Ceylon tea plantation and factory
    • Witness a colourful Kandyan cultural performance
    • Visit the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic in Kandy
    • Climb the magnificent Sigiriya Rock Fortress
    • Visit Thirukoneswaram Temple and unwind at Nilaveli Beach
    • Visit Keerimalai Springs, Naguleswaram Temple and Nallur Kandaswamy Temple
    • Join local fishermen for a traditional fishing and marine experience
    • Palmyrah toddy tasting, pottery-making and a Jaffna cooking class

    Your route

    1. 01Colombo & Negombo
    2. 02Galle Fort
    3. 03Mirissa & Ahangama
    4. 04Yala National Park
    5. 05Ella
    6. 06Nuwara Eliya & tea country
    7. 07Kandy
    8. 08Sigiriya
    9. 09Trincomalee
    10. 10Jaffna

    Day by day

    Itinerary

    Day 01Ayubowan! Welcome Home – Airport to Colombo

    A warm airport welcome, meet your chauffeur-guide and transfer to Colombo. The rest of the day is free to settle in.

    Stay: GranBell Hotel Colombo (4 Star) or SimilarActivity level: Easy

    Day 02Colombo – Galle Fort – Mirissa

    Walk the ramparts, lighthouse and streets of UNESCO-listed Galle Fort, then continue to a beachside hotel.

    Stay: Insight Resort Ahangama (4 Star) or SimilarMeals: BreakfastActivity level: Easy to Medium

    Day 03Leisure DayMirissa

    A free family day: beach, optional beginner surfing, pool club, Coconut Tree Hill and Parrot Rock.

    Stay: Insight Resort Ahangama (4 Star) or SimilarMeals: BreakfastActivity level: Easy

    Day 04Mirissa – Yala National Park Safari – Ella

    Private jeep safari in Yala (sightings depend on natural conditions), then onward to Ella.

    Stay: 88 th Ella (3 Star) or Similar

    Day 05Little Adam’s Peak, Nine Arch Bridge & Pool ClubElla

    Optional sunrise hike to Little Adam’s Peak, Nine Arch Bridge and an afternoon at Ravana Pool Club.

    Stay: 88 th Ella (3 Star) or Similar

    Day 06Scenic Train Journey – Ella to Nanu Oya – Nuwara Eliya

    Hill-country train from Ella to Nanu Oya (tickets subject to availability), then a short Nuwara Eliya city tour.

    Stay: Highland Stone House (4 Star) or Similar

    Day 07Nuwara Eliya – Ramboda – Kandy

    Ramboda Falls, Ramboda Hanuman Temple, a tea plantation and factory, a Kandyan Cultural Dance Show and the Temple of the Tooth Relic.

    Stay: Radisson Hotel Kandy (4 Star) or Similar

    Day 08Kandy – Spice Garden – Sigiriya Village Experience

    A spice garden, a Sigiriya village experience with a traditional lunch, and an evening Sigiriya Rock climb.

    Day 09Sigiriya – Trincomalee Cultural & Coastal Experience

    Thirukoneswaram Temple, Kanniya Hot Water Springs and an evening at Nilaveli Beach.

    Day 10Trincomalee – Journey to Jaffna – Nallur Temple

    Keerimalai Hot Springs and Naguleswaram Temple en route, then the Nallur Kandaswamy Temple in the evening.

    Stay: Hotel Sangilian (4 Star) or SimilarMeals: BreakfastActivity level: Easy

    Day 11Fishing & Marine ExperienceJaffna

    A boat trip with local fishermen, a fresh seafood breakfast in a palmyrah-roofed sea setting, and an evening city tour with Jaffna Fort.

    Stay: Hotel Sangilian (4 Star) or SimilarMeals: BreakfastActivity level: Easy to Moderate

    Day 12Toddy, Pottery & Cooking ClassJaffna

    A palmyrah toddy farm, a traditional pottery-making session and an authentic Jaffna cooking class with dinner.

    Stay: Hotel Sangilian (4 Star) or SimilarMeals: Breakfast and Dinner

    Day 13Last Moments in Jaffna – Journey to Colombo

    A free morning, then the long drive to Colombo through Elephant Pass.

    Stay: Granbell Hotel Colombo (4 Star) or Similar

    Day 14Departure from Sri Lanka

    Final leisure time and a private transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport.

    Activity level: Easy

    Hotels are listed “or similar” as published. Itinerary summarised from the company’s published tour page.
