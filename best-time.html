    Best time to visit

    There’s always a sunny coast.

    Sri Lanka has two monsoons that hit opposite sides of the island — so the right region beats the “right” month.

    Month by month

    An indicative weather guide.

    Indicative only — weather varies year to year. Based on the sources below.

    West & south coast

    Driest and sunniest from December to March. The southwest monsoon brings rain from May to September.

    Hill country

    Also at its best December to March; cooler, with temperatures down to around 14°C. Heaviest rain usually June–August.

    East coast

    Finest from May to September, when the west is wet. The northeast monsoon arrives from around October.

    North (Jaffna)

    Monsoon rain roughly October to January; July and August are festival season, so book early.

    The two monsoons

    How the seasons work

    • May – SeptemberSouthwest monsoon: wetter west, south and hill country; the east coast is typically dry.
    • October – NovemberInter-monsoon: thunderstorms possible across the island.
    • December – FebruaryNortheast monsoon: rain in the north and east; west, south and hills are driest.
    • March – AprilInter-monsoon: warm, with afternoon showers possible.
    • November – AprilWhale-watching season on the south coast, typically — sea conditions decide on the day.
