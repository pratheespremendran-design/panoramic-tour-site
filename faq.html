    FAQ

    Questions, answered honestly.

    Can’t find what you need? WhatsApp us on +94 77 419 2370.

    How do I book a journey?

    Share your plans through our trip planner, WhatsApp or email. A local expert replies with a tailored itinerary and quote. Your booking is confirmed once the 50% advance is received.

    Are the prices on the website final?

    No. They are “from” prices. The final quote depends on travel dates, group size and hotel category.

    What are the payment terms?

    50% advance to confirm, with the balance due 30 days before arrival. Payment details are shared directly by our team after confirmation and are never published on this website.

    What is the cancellation policy?

    Cancellations made more than 30 days before arrival carry a 15% fee. Bookings cancelled within 30 days of arrival are 100% non-refundable. Approved refunds are processed within 7–14 business days.

    Can I customise a journey?

    Yes — every journey is tailor-made. Hotels, pace, activities and extensions can be adjusted to suit your style and budget.

    What’s usually included?

    Inclusions vary by journey and are listed on each journey page — typically airport transfers, transport with an English-speaking chauffeur guide, daily breakfast and 24/7 local assistance. Airfare and insurance are not included.

    Are train seats guaranteed on the scenic train ride?

    Train tickets are included where listed, but seat class depends on availability. If reserved seating is unavailable, an alternative class or a road transfer will be arranged.

    Will we see leopards or whales?

    Wildlife is never guaranteed. Leopards live in Yala but sightings vary; whale watching off Mirissa is typically best November to April and depends on sea conditions.

    When is the best time to visit?

    It depends on the region: the west, south and hill country are driest December–March; the east coast is best May–September. See our month-by-month guide.

    Do I need a visa?

    Most visitors need an approved Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) before travelling. Rules change, so please check the official Sri Lanka immigration website for your nationality before you book flights.

    What should I wear at temples?

    Cover shoulders and knees, and remove shoes and hats before entering. Follow any photography rules on site.

    Can I book or pay on this website?

    No. This website has no live booking or payment system. Use the planner, WhatsApp or email and our team will guide you personally.
