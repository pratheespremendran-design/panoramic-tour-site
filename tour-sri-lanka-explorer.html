    Overview

    A diverse journey combining ancient UNESCO heritage, authentic village experiences, sacred cities, misty hill country, scenic rail travel, wildlife and beautiful tropical beaches.

    From Negombo into the Cultural Triangle (Dambulla, Sigiriya, Polonnaruwa), through Matale to Kandy, by train into tea country and Ella, then Yala, two nights in Mirissa, Galle Fort and Bentota.

    Highlights

    • A warm Ayubowan welcome and coastal stay in Negombo
    • The UNESCO-listed Dambulla Cave Temple
    • Sigiriya Village Tour with bullock-cart and catamaran rides
    • A traditional Sri Lankan cooking demonstration & village lunch
    • Climb the magnificent Sigiriya Rock Fortress
    • The Ancient City of Polonnaruwa, including Gal Vihara
    • Matale Spice Garden and the Temple of the Tooth Relic
    • Kandy Lake, Kandy Viewpoint & a Kandyan Cultural Dance Show
    • Tea Plantation & Tea Factory with Ceylon Tea tasting
    • The Nanu Oya to Ella scenic train journey
    • Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam’s Peak and Ravana Falls
    • Yala National Park Jeep Safari (own expense)
    • Mirissa Beach, Parrot Rock & Coconut Tree Hill
    • UNESCO-listed Galle Fort & Galle Lighthouse
    • Bentota Beach with an optional river safari

    Good to know

    Journey FAQs

    How demanding is this tour physically?

    Mostly low to medium, with a few medium/high moments: the Sigiriya Rock Fortress climb and the Little Adam’s Peak hike. If anyone prefers a lighter pace, you can skip or shorten the hikes.

    Will we definitely get the scenic train seats from Nanu Oya to Ella?

    Train tickets are included, but seat class depends on availability. If reserved seats aren’t available, you may travel in an available class or switch to a comfortable road transfer.

    What is the best season for Mirissa whale watching?

    Whale watching is seasonal, typically best from November to April on the south coast.

    Can we change Yala to Udawalawe?

    Yes. If your priority is elephants and easier viewing, Udawalawe is often preferred; for leopard chances and dramatic landscapes, Yala is the classic choice.

    Can we customize hotels?

    Yes, this itinerary is easy to upgrade. You can swap each hotel category while keeping the same route and activities.
